📖 Reading a message-exchange diagram: who sends what to whom?

Every OCPP conversation is drawn the same way: two people facing each other. On the left the charging station 🔌, on the right the CSMS 🖥️ (the backend). Time flows downwards. Each horizontal arrow is one message.

How to read one arrow

  1. Who sends: where the arrow starts. Who receives: where the arrowhead points.
  2. What kind: the colour and the number in brackets (the first element of the JSON array).
  3. About what: the action name on a request (BootNotification, Heartbeat…).
  4. Which question it answers: the id. An answer always copies the id of its request.
  5. Business "no" ≠ error: "status: Rejected" is a normal green answer. Red means the request itself was broken or not allowed.

The five kinds of arrow

📨CALL [2]: asksa request; the other side must answer
[2, id, Action, {…}]
✅CALLRESULT [3]: answerssame id as the request
[3, id, {…}]
❌CALLERROR [4]: refusesthe request was broken / not allowed
[4, id, code, text, {…}]
⛔CALLRESULTERROR [5]2.1 only: "your answer was broken"
[5, id, code, text, {…}]
📣SEND [6]: tells2.1 only: never answered
[6, id, Action, {…}]

In the Live tab

🖱️ Hover an arrow: its request/answer partner lights up. Click it: a panel explains who sent it to whom, what it means, what it answers, and every field. The pictures on the left show what each side believes: the station's screen, the link to the CSMS, cars on the cables.
